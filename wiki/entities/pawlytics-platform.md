# Pawlytics Platform

**Pawlytics** is a stray animal analytics and conflict intelligence web application designed for citizens and municipal authorities.

## Core Capabilities
- Citizen incident reporting & live safe route navigation.
- Municipal authority control room with verification queue and hotspot analysis.
- Full role-based authority settings and GIS data exports.
